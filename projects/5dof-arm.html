5DOF Robotic Arm

A five-degree-of-freedom robotic arm, with a staged roadmap from manual control to vision-guided manipulation and learned actions.

Project focus

5 DOF3D printingArduinoPython

Under development · planned V1–V5 progression

Overview

Designing a custom, 3D-printed manipulator inspired by the SO-101 architecture, with five arm degrees of freedom and an actuated gripper. The design emphasizes modular mechanics, servo-actuated joints, an Arduino and PCA9685 control architecture, and encoder-ready joint housings. ROS2 integration and robot-learning methods are future directions.
